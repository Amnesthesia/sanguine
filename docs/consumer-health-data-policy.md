# Sanguine Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

## 1. Scope and Core Commitments
This Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy applies to personal health data collected through the Sanguine mobile application on iOS and Android. It addresses requirements under United States state health privacy laws, including the Washington My Health My Data Act (MHMDA), Nevada SB 370, and the Connecticut Data Privacy Act (CTDPA).

Sanguine is an on-device cycle tracker. It is not a contraceptive and is not a medical device. Sanguine does not identify conditions, provide medical opinions, or substitute for professional medical care.

Data Controller: the Sanguine developer. Contact: admin@dothings.lol.

## 2. Categories of Consumer Health Data Collected
All data in Sanguine is entered directly by you or imported with your explicit permission. Categories include:
- Menstrual cycle information: Dates of bleeding, flow intensity, and cycle history.
- Biomarker and physiological data: Basal body temperature, cervical mucus consistency, and ovulation test results.
- Self-reported symptoms: Mood, energy, pain indicators, and cycle-related notes.
- Optional sexual activity records: Self-reported dates and occurrences, kept disabled by default.

Sanguine does not collect biometric identifiers, genetic data, precise location records, or general internet activity.

## 3. Purpose of Processing Consumer Health Data
Sanguine processes consumer health data solely for the primary purpose of providing the service: calculating cycle estimates, displaying history, and rendering local calendar views.

Processing takes place entirely on your device. Sanguine operates without user accounts, maintains no server infrastructure, and includes no analytics SDKs, crash-reporting trackers, or advertising libraries. The developer cannot view, harvest, or monetise your health data.

## 4. No Sharing or Disclosure
Sanguine does not share, disclose, or transfer consumer health data to third parties, corporate affiliates, or advertising networks.

- Optional backups: If you enable encrypted cloud backup, an encrypted archive is written directly to your personal iCloud Drive or Google Drive application folder. Encryption uses Argon2id and XChaCha20-Poly1305. The developer holds no keys and cannot read or inspect this archive.
- Your own phones: if you link a second phone with your recovery code, each phone merges the entries in that encrypted archive before it uploads a new one. The archive stays in your own cloud folder; nothing passes through the developer.
- Optional health sync: Sanguine can sync with Apple Health or Health Connect. Synchronisation is off by default and needs your consent per data type, given in Settings and confirmed by the operating system. Transferred data is governed by the platform operating system policies. A backup never contains records that were only imported from Apple Health.

## 5. No Sale of Consumer Health Data
Sanguine has never sold, licensed, rented, or traded consumer health data, and will never sell consumer health data for money or any other valuable consideration.

## 6. Prohibition on Geofencing
Sanguine does not use geofencing technology. Sanguine never establishes virtual perimeters around medical facilities, reproductive healthcare clinics, or any other physical locations to identify, track, or collect data from consumers. Sanguine does not request or access device GPS coordinates.

## 7. Consumer Rights and How to Exercise Them
Consumers under Washington MHMDA, Nevada SB 370, Connecticut CTDPA, and similar statutes have distinct rights regarding their health data:
- Right to confirm and access: You can confirm what data is stored and obtain a copy. In Sanguine, tap Settings -> Privacy and data -> Export to generate a complete JSON or CSV file.
- Right to withdraw consent: You can withdraw consent at any time by ceasing tracking or toggling off individual tracking types in Settings.
- Right to deletion: You can delete your consumer health data at any time. Selecting "Delete everything" in Settings -> Privacy and data immediately wipes all database records from your phone.
- Right to appeal: Because all processing occurs on-device and the developer retains no personal data on servers, rights are exercised directly inside the app. If you believe a request has not been fulfilled or wish to submit an appeal regarding your consumer health data rights, email: admin@dothings.lol. Appeals will be reviewed within 45 days.

## 8. Age Limits and Teen Settings
Sanguine requires users to be at least 13 years of age. For users aged 13 to 17, the application automatically enables teen mode. Teen mode hides sexual activity tracking, suppresses fertility estimate cards, and sets cycle thresholds appropriate for younger individuals.
