Sanguine · glass design language

Glass for navigation (toolbars, tab bar, sheets, floating controls); opaque content underneath. Solid means you logged it; dashed or soft-glow means it is an estimate. One filled control per screen. Everything round.

Glass: blur 18 with saturation 200%, so what scrolls under it stays visible but soft; a thin white tint brighter at the top edge (specular), a light rim, tight + soft shadow. In the app this is the system material (expo-glass-effect), which also adapts text vibrancy. Floating glass that holds text over scrolling content (Log bar, day bar, open sheet) adds a legibility tint so nothing beneath reads through. The filled primary is white ink on accentFill (dark: the darker #BB546B). Content cards never glass.
