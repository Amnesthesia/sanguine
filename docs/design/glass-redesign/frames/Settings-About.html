How to read estimates

Every estimate is a range with a confidence word. A darker fertile-window band means more likely to be in the window; it never marks a single day. Estimates change when you log more. Symptoms never change an estimate.

How Sanguine estimatesHow the ranges are worked out, what the confidence words mean and what changes them. ›
period
Days of menstrual bleeding.
bleeding
Any blood loss logged, including spotting and bleeding outside a period.
spotting
Very light bleeding, less than a period.
cycle
From the first day of one period to the day before the next.
estimate
A calculated guess shown with a range, never a certainty.
range
The span of days an estimate is likely to fall in.
ovulation
When an egg is released. Sanguine estimates this day and shows a range.
fertile window
The days around ovulation shown with a likelihood. Darker means more likely to be in the window.
biomarker
A measurable sign such as temperature, an ovulation test or cervical mucus.
