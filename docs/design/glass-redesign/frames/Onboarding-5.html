Your data stays with you

Everything you log stays on this phone, encrypted. Sanguine has no account and no server; we cannot see your data and cannot hand it to anyone. Backups, if you turn them on, are encrypted before they reach your iCloud or Google Drive.

You can lock Sanguine with Face ID, your fingerprint or your phone's passcode in Settings, Privacy and data.

Health sync opens when you finish. Nothing is switched on until you choose it.

You can set up an encrypted backup in Settings, under Privacy and data.
