## Description

Sanguine helps you track and understand your menstrual cycle. It is not a contraceptive and is not a medical device.

Everything you log stays on your phone, encrypted. Sanguine has no account and no server, so we cannot see your data and cannot hand it to anyone.

- Log your period, flow, symptoms, temperature, ovulation tests and cervical mucus in two taps.
- See your next period and your ovulation as ranges, with a plain-language confidence grade, never as a single promised day.
- Understand your history: cycle length, period length and how they vary.
- Import your history from Natural Cycles or a CSV, and export everything whenever you like.
- Optional two-way sync with Apple Health and Health Connect, one data type at a time.
- Optional encrypted backup to your own iCloud Drive or Google Drive. The file is encrypted on your phone before it leaves.
- App lock, discreet notifications, a discreet app icon and name, and screenshot protection.
- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Free, with nothing locked away.

Sanguine does not diagnose, screen for or treat any condition. If you are worried about your health, talk to a health professional.

## Privacy answers (D-072)

Nothing the user logs ever leaves the phone except in the optional backup, which is encrypted on the phone first. The one thing that does leave is the update check (D-069): on each launch expo-updates sends Expo's update service a random installation id (`EAS-Client-ID`), the app and runtime version and the platform, and the request carries the IP address. Expo keeps the id to count updated installs, so it counts as collected, and the answers declare it.

- App Store "App Privacy": Data collected: Identifiers > Device ID. Purpose: App Functionality. Linked to the user: No. Used for tracking: No. Every other category: not collected (health and fitness data, sensitive info, contact info, usage data, diagnostics, location). The privacy manifest in `app.json` declares the same and `tools/ci/store.test.ts` keeps them in step.
- Google Play "Data safety": Data collected: Device or other IDs. Shared: No. Processed ephemerally: No. Required (users cannot turn it off): Yes. Purpose: App functionality. Encrypted in transit: Yes (HTTPS). Deletion: users delete everything Sanguine holds on the phone in Settings > Privacy and data > Delete everything; the installation id at Expo is not linked to them and is replaced on reinstall. Health and fitness data: not collected (it never leaves the device; the optional Google Drive backup goes to the user's own app-data folder, end-to-end encrypted before upload; Google's form exempts end-to-end encrypted data the developer cannot read).
- Health declarations: Apple usage strings are in `app.json`; the Google Play health apps declaration and the Health Connect data-type declaration text is in `docs/store/play-health-declaration.md`.
- Export compliance: `app.json` sets `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` false. The backup uses standard published algorithms (XChaCha20-Poly1305, Argon2id) for the user's own data; confirm this answer in App Store Connect on the first submission.
