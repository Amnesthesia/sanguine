# Name, logo and brand

The Sanguine source code is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (`LICENSE`); `packages/engine` is MIT (`packages/engine/LICENSE`). Those licences cover code, not the brand.

The name "Sanguine", the emblem, the script logotype, the splash and icon artwork (`docs/brand-v2/`, `apps/mobile/assets/brand/`) and the six taglines are the project's brand assets. They are not licensed for reuse under the AGPL. If you publish a fork or a modified build, give it a different name and different artwork, and do not suggest that it is the official app.

You may use the name to say truthfully that your work is based on Sanguine, and you may quote the fixed wording in `copy/legal.en-AU.json` only together with the whole disclaimer it belongs to.

Trademark registration, and the owner's decision on who holds the brand, are not settled in this repository: see `docs/PROGRESS.md`, "Needs human".
